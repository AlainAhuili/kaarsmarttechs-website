These sculptures combine metal craftsmanship with energy autonomy. Miniature photovoltaic cells charge a battery during the day, and at dusk a small controller lights up LED constellations along the wireframe.

## Design goals

- **Miniature photovoltaic cells** built into the sculpture.
- **Weatherproof welded wireframe** structures for outdoor use.
- **Dusk-to-dawn control** that switches the lighting on and off with the ambient light.
